ABOUT ME

Student. Artist. Creator.

I’m B SAILAKSHANTH, the creator behind Silent Strokes.

I’m a student who enjoys balancing my studies with my passion for drawing and creativity. I love creating anime art, sketches, illustrations, and drawing videos.

My studies help me build knowledge and skills for the future, while art gives me a space to explore ideas, experiment, and express myself.

Every drawing is another opportunity to learn something new, improve my skills, and keep moving forward.
