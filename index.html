FROM MY JOURNAL

The Blog.

01 / CREATIVE JOURNEY

How I Started Drawing

A short look at how my interest in drawing began and how it became part of my creative journey.

READ ARTICLE → CLOSE ARTICLE ↑

For me, drawing is a way to explore ideas and characters I enjoy. Anime art and sketches give me room to practise, notice expressions and shapes, and work toward the image I have in mind.

I still have plenty to learn, and every page gives me something new to try. Silent Strokes is where I share that progress and the creative experiments along the way.

02 / ART

My Approach to Anime Art

Exploring the ideas, inspiration, and techniques behind the anime artwork I create.

READ ARTICLE → CLOSE ARTICLE ↑

Anime characters are full of expression, and that is what I enjoy exploring in a drawing. I pay attention to the shapes and mood I want to show, then build the details around that idea.

Each piece gives me a chance to experiment with line, proportion, and shading. I keep adjusting and learning as I go, aiming to make artwork that feels expressive and personal.

03 / STUDENT LIFE

Balancing Studies and Creativity

How I make time for creativity while continuing to focus on school and learning.

READ ARTICLE → CLOSE ARTICLE ↑

Studying and drawing both matter to me. Some days need more attention for schoolwork; at other times I can make space for a sketch. I try to keep moving forward in both, even when the steps are small.

Studies help me build knowledge for the future, while art gives me a way to explore ideas and express myself. Making room for both helps me keep learning and creating.

New journal entry · Creative practice

Finding Ideas in a Sketch

Not every drawing starts with a complete plan. Sometimes I begin with a few loose lines and let the idea take shape as I go. A sketch gives me room to explore expressions, shapes, and details before I decide what the finished piece should become.

READ ARTICLE → CLOSE ARTICLE ↑

If a line does not work, I can change it. If something feels right, I build on it. That process helps me learn with every drawing and keeps creating fun.

I share anime art, sketches, illustrations, and videos here because each piece is part of that journey—one stroke at a time.
